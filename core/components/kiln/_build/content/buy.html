How a Pro licence works

  • Your licence key arrives by email as soon as your payment goes through, and it’s kept on your account page.
  • Paste it into Settings → License in Kiln. That’s it: no reinstall, and your sites stay as they are.
  • A licence is for one person, on all of that person’s own computers. Buying for a team? Each member gets their own key.
  • Annual plans and teams renew each year until you cancel. If you stop, your key keeps working to the end of the year you paid for.
